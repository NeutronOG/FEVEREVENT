# FEVER media

Replace `fever-atmosphere.webp` with approved FEVER photography, or add compressed
AVIF/WebP images and muted MP4/WebM clips here. Recommended subjects: DJ booth,
crowd silhouettes, hands in the air, amber lighting, venue details, and blurred
club moments.

Keep portrait images near 1600 × 2400, full-width images near 2400 × 1600, and
short video loops under 4 MB where possible. Preserve the filenames used by the
